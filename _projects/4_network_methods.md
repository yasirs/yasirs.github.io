A thread through my work is building methods that exploit the structure of biological networks and the statistics of noisy, mixed datasets.

- A graph diffusion kernel for predicting novel genetic interactions and co-complex membership in yeast ([Genome Research 2008](/publications/)).
- Statistical models of intercellular transfer of biomolecules and of paracrine secretion dynamics, with information-theoretic bounds on the variance.
- Correcting testing bias in estimates of SARS-CoV-2 epidemic dynamics ([BMC Medical Research Methodology 2021](/publications/)).
- Signal processing for neural spike recordings from implantable electrode arrays (IEEE, 2004-2007).
