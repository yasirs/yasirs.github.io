Placental trophoblasts and cancer cells both invade stroma, and the strength of stromal resistance has evolved differently across mammals. I compare transcriptomes and gene regulatory mechanisms across species to find the genes behind this resistance.

- Cis-regulatory changes underlying endometrial control of placental invasion ([PNAS 2022](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2111256119)).
- Evolved resistance to placental invasion and survival in melanoma patients.
- Coopetition at the maternal-fetal interface shaping placental invasion ([PNAS 2025](/publications/)).
- Scar matrix, Piezo1 and stromal inflammation in placenta accreta spectrum ([Nature Communications 2024](/publications/)), with spatial transcriptomics of patient tissue.
