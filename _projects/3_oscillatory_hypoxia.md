Tumors experience oxygen levels that rise and fall rather than staying constant. I use bioinformatics and mathematical models to study how oscillatory HIF-1 activity changes gene expression in cancer cells and fibroblasts.

- Oscillatory hypoxia induced gene expression predicts low survival in breast cancer patients ([Molecular Carcinogenesis 2024](/publications/)).
- Feed-forward loop models explaining the transcriptional response to cycling hypoxia ([npj Systems Biology and Applications 2025](/publications/)).
- Lactate-dependent chaperone-mediated autophagy and oscillatory HIF-1α activity ([Cell Systems 2022](/publications/)).
