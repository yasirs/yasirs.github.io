Pre-eclampsia and fetal growth disorders are hard to predict early. This project builds prognostic models from the transcriptional state of stromal cells at the maternal-fetal interface.

- Semi-supervised learning on large single-cell RNAseq datasets.
- A variational autoencoder generative model that relates fibroblast transcriptional states to systemic disease states.
- Graph neural networks over spatial single-cell transcriptomics from patient tissue samples, integrating transcriptional profiles, genetic perturbations and cellular phenotype assays.

Funded by an NICHD R00 (2025-2028, sole PI), following a K99 (2022-2025) on mapping trophoblast-decidual signaling to its effects on invasion-related maternal-fetal diseases.
