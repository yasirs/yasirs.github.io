Co-authored paper in [Molecular Cell](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2026.03.007): in pancreatic cancer, tumor fluid together with low oxygen protects cells from ferroptosis, an iron-dependent form of cell death, and the factor HIF-2 coordinates that protection.
