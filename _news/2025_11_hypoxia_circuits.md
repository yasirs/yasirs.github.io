Last-author paper in [npj Systems Biology and Applications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41540-025-00612-z): simple gene-circuit models show how a cell can respond to cycling hypoxia differently from stable hypoxia, and experiments point to p53 and Notch1 as partners of HIF-1.
