Co-authored paper in [Nature Communications](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52351-0): scar matrix from cesarean surgery activates the mechanosensitive channel Piezo1 in uterine stromal cells, triggering inflammatory signals that draw trophoblasts in, a mechanism behind placenta accreta spectrum.
